### Mian Dai

Postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Materials Science, TU Darmstadt.
I work on AI for materials science, connecting first-principles simulation with semantic data:
density functional theory and Bayesian optimization on one side, EMMO-based ontologies and
knowledge graphs on the other.

**Research**

- **Materials ontologies.** Domain ontologies within [EMMO](https://emmo-repo.github.io/) that give
  electronic, atomistic, mesoscopic and continuum simulations of magnetic materials a common description.
- **Active learning and Bayesian optimization.** Automated construction of effective Hamiltonians,
  and multi-objective optimization over high-dimensional alloy composition spaces.
- **High-throughput first-principles workflows.** EMTO-CPA and VASP pipelines for high-entropy alloys
  and functional materials.
